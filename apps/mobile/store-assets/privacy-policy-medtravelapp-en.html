OYSGroup S.A. ("we", "us") develops and operates MedTravelApp, an application for travelers that centralizes their medical record, coverage information, and emergency assistance while traveling. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it.

1. What data we collect

CategoryData included
Account data First and last name, email, phone number, ID document, profile photo (optional)
Health data (special category data) Allergies, medical conditions, medications, surgeries, implants/devices, vital signs (weight, height, blood pressure), blood type, lab results/studies, emergency contacts
Coverage data Travel insurance policy, associated provider/insurer
Travel data Destinations, travel dates, trip status (planned/completed)
Location The country/destination you indicate when adding a trip, and — only if you authorize it — your location when reporting an emergency, to speed up assistance
Conversations with the AI assistant The messages you type or say by voice to the health/emergency assistant while completing your medical record or requesting help
Technical usage data Access logs, device identifier, and metadata necessary for the app's operation and security (e.g. active session)

2. What we use your data for

  • Showing and keeping your Health Record up to date within the app.
  • Generating a link or QR code so that a doctor treating you during a trip can view your relevant clinical information, only when you decide to share it.
  • Coordinating assistance during a medical emergency while traveling, including communicating with an assistance case opened by our operations team.
  • Offering a conversational assistant (powered by artificial intelligence) to help you complete your medical record faster, by text or by voice.
  • Verifying your identity and your associated coverage.
  • Complying with legal and information security obligations.

3. Who we share your data with

  • Healthcare professionals you choose: only when you generate a share link/QR code for your Health Record, or during an active emergency with an open case.
  • Our operations team: authorized staff who manage assistance and coverage cases, under restricted and audited access.
  • Infrastructure and artificial intelligence providers: we use third-party services (e.g. OpenAI) to process assistant conversations and, for health data, to interpret and structure what you write or say — these providers process the information on our behalf under their own confidentiality agreements, do not use it to train their models on your data, and do not sell it to third parties.
  • We never sell your data to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

4. How we protect your data

  • Health data and other sensitive personal data are stored encrypted in our database.
  • Access to clinical information is restricted: it is only enabled when there is your active consent or an open emergency case linked to you.
  • Communications between the app and our servers travel encrypted (HTTPS).

5. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access the data we hold about you directly from the app ("Health", "Profile").
  • Correct incorrectly loaded data, directly from the app or by requesting it through the contact channels below.
  • Request the deletion of your account and your data, except for information we are legally required to retain (e.g. records of assistance already provided).
  • Revoke a doctor's access to a shared link/QR code by deactivating it from the app.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you request your account be closed, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except for information we must retain due to legal or contractual obligations (e.g. records tied to an active insurance policy).

7. Minors

MedTravelApp is not intended for use by minors without the supervision of a responsible adult. If a minor travels under coverage, their medical record must be loaded and managed by their parent or legal guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make significant updates to this policy, we will notify you within the app before the change takes effect.
